Hi! I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Industrial and Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Raed Al Kontar.

Prior to that, I received my undergraduate education in Industrial and Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan and Electrical and Computer Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I am broadly interested in the intersection of machine learning, statistics, and optimization. My current interests are Bayesian optimization and diffusion models.

Understanding Generalization in Diffusion Models via Probability Flow Distance

Huijie Zhang, Zijian Huang, Siyi Chen, Jinfan Zhou, Zekai Zhang, Peng Wang, Qing Qu

ArXiv Website All Publications

We propose Probability Flow Distance (PFD), a theoretically grounded and computationally efficient metric to quantify distributional generalization in diffusion models. Using a teacher-student evaluation protocol, we empirically uncover several novel generalization behaviors.
